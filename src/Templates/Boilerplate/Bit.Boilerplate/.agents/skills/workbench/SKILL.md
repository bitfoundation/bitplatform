---
name: workbench
description: Designs and reviews a page's UI and UX in the Workbench (`src/Workbench`) before it is built into the app: the page runs on the app's own bit BlazorUI components and theme with in-memory sample data, is checked on phones, tablets and desktops in light, dark and right-to-left with every data state, and is captured as pixel-exact screenshots, including App Store, Google Play and Microsoft Store images. Use when the user wants to design, mock up or prototype a page or a UI/UX idea, compare a page across devices, themes, languages or data states, make store screenshots, or has agreed to design a new page in the Workbench first.
